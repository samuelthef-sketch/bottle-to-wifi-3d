      The pieces add up to about 2.9 m², almost a full 4 × 8 ft sheet (3.0 m²), and they can't all be laid out on one sheet. Buy 2 sheets, or 1 sheet plus a half sheet if your lumber yard sells halves. This replaces the "1 sheet" in the materials list.

      Building tips

      • Cut the chute hole with a 4½" (114 mm) hole saw; that is the outside size of 4" PVC.
      • Join panels with wood glue and 1¼" wood screws, pre-drilled.
      • Seal every face and edge with marine varnish; put the machine under a roof or awning.
      • WiFi passes through thin plywood with little loss, so the router can stay inside.
